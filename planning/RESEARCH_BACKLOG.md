## Research standard

Every completed study records hypothesis, participant/sample definition, segment coverage, consent and compensation, accommodations, data/recording retention, method, limitations, raw evidence location, synthesis, saturation/decision rule, outcome, and planning changes. A preference is not promoted to a requirement merely because it appeared in one interview. Research involving disability-related information applies the RSR requirements in the PRD.
