## Per-expedition definition of done

Observable objective; canonical coverage; exact provenance and review date; technical, editorial, and accessibility review; checkpoint or justified omission; passing lab assertions; accessible fallback; performance budget; supported-matrix evidence; coverage update; and no unresolved high-severity defect.

WP-501–WP-504 are wave containers, not issue-ready tasks. Before activation, decompose each into independently reviewable expedition planning, Journey content, Atlas records, lab/Browser Lens work, integration, cross-discipline review, and gate-decision packages with exact requirement IDs.
