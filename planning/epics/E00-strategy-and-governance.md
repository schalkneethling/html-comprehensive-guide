# E00 — Strategy and Governance

## Outcome

An agreed product contract and execution system keeps research, content, design, and engineering aligned.

## Scope

Planning authority, success measures, decisions, risks, delivery rules, review roles, and traceability. This epic does not choose detailed product architecture.

## Work packages

| ID | Work package | Requirements | Dependencies | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WP-000 | Ratify goal, vision, principles, and fresh-start boundary | All goals | None | Maintainer review; contradictions resolved across planning docs |
| WP-001 | Confirm audience, scope assumptions, and non-goals to test | G1–G6 | WP-000 | Research hypotheses and decision rules documented |
| WP-002 | Establish requirement IDs and traceability workflow | All | WP-000 | Every requirement family maps to an epic and verification type |
| WP-003 | Establish decision and risk governance | `REL-*` | WP-000 | Decision template, risk ownership, gate review process accepted |
| WP-004 | Define measurement framework | G1–G6 | WP-001 | Each measure has method, baseline plan, target-setting rule, and privacy boundary |
| WP-005 | Define delivery model and GitHub issue lifecycle | All | WP-002–004 | Definition of ready/done, issue template, review roles, change policy documented |

## Gate

All planning artifacts agree; uncertain choices are labelled; active-phase risks have owners; and Phase 1 can begin without relying on an unresolved product premise.
