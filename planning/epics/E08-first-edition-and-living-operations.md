# E08 — First Edition and Living Operations

## Outcome

A frozen first-edition scope is fully reviewed as a dated edition, wider product scope remains visible, and the continuously updated product has credible human and automated maintenance operations.

## Work packages

| ID | Work package | Requirements | Dependencies | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WP-800 | Freeze and reach 100% reviewed first-edition scope | ATL-05, CNT-01–05, EDN-01–02 | E05 | Every unit frozen into the edition is reviewed; wider scope accounting is reported separately |
| WP-801 | Complete integrated technical, editorial, accessibility, security, privacy, and performance audit | All applicable | WP-800 | Findings resolved or explicitly accepted below release threshold |
| WP-802 | Generate accessible static/downloadable reading edition | REL-01, A11Y-01–05, EDN-03 | WP-801 | Interactive concepts retain explanations and links; navigation and media accessibility pass |
| WP-803 | Audit and harden anchor checking and source-change candidate detection | ATL-02, CNT-05 | E02, E04, WP-800 | Operations since public foundation produce actionable, low-noise review queues without auto-rewriting claims |
| WP-804 | Establish human freshness triage and correction service levels | CNT-03–05 | WP-803 | Ownership, priority, escalation, review authority, and stale-content presentation exercised |
| WP-805 | Publish provenance, freshness dashboard, errata, and changelog | ATL-05, CNT-05 | WP-803–804 | Public states match source records and corrections are traceable |
| WP-806 | Establish dated snapshots and quarterly field reports | G4, G6, EDN-03 | WP-800–805 | Reproducible edition process and first change-report dry run complete |
| WP-807 | Conduct first-edition go/hold review | EDN-01–03 | WP-800–806 | Evidence-based release decision and post-release risk/operations review recorded |

Accounts and the Navigator are excluded from the first-edition critical path unless a separate accepted decision adds them to that edition's frozen scope.
