# E01 — Discovery, Scope, and Content Architecture

## Outcome

The project has evidence-backed audience boundaries, a measurable definition of comprehensive, a complete current-content disposition, and a coherent Journey/Atlas/Laboratory model.

## Scope

Research and information architecture. No production shell or large-scale rewrite.

## Gate

The Phase 1 exit gate in the roadmap is satisfied. There is no unaccounted existing content; the taxonomy works on a representative cross-section; and full seeding has an executable plan. Full disposition is required before a comprehensiveness claim, not before the First Byte experiment.
